WP-01 · PRODUCT & LEAD ENGINEER ~1 WEEK → MINUTES

ViiV Finance Commentary Tool

Led a 4-person cross-functional pod (UX, UI, architecture, PM) to ship a secure, role-aware finance platform that lets Country, Regional, and Global leaders self-serve monthly P&L, SG&A, and headcount variance commentary, compressing a week-long manual cycle to minutes. Databricks Genie "Ask-AI" assistant + SQL Warehouse dashboards, entitlement-based RBAC, OIDC/JWKS auth, query caching, and a Modify → Validate → Finalize workflow with automated roll-ups.

IMPACT → Country, Regional, and Global finance leaders now self-serve their monthly commentary in minutes instead of a week, and Global Finance can explore insights without waiting on Country or Region submissions.
FastAPI React · Vite Databricks Genie SQL Warehouse RBAC · OIDC
WP-02 · ARCHITECT & FULL-STACK SELF-SERVE ANALYTICS

BISON: Conversational NL-to-SQL

A conversational NL-to-SQL assistant answering finance questions (turnover, SG&A, plan-vs-actual, YoY/QoQ) over a Databricks warehouse via an 8-phase LangGraph pipeline: follow-up detection, entity resolution (Azure AI Search), template matching, SQL generation/execution, and answer synthesis with memory. Shipped full-stack with an interactive-chart chat UI, CSV export, SSO, a 30+ scenario Playwright eval/regression suite, and Dockerized CI/CD.

IMPACT → Finance business users answer their own turnover, SG&A, and plan-vs-actual questions on demand, removing the analyst bottleneck and cutting time-to-answer from hours to seconds.
LangGraph GPT-4o Next.js Azure AI Search Playwright
WP-03 · AGENTIC PLATFORM WEEKS → MINUTES

Vaccine & HIV Demand Forecasting

Architected an agentic forecasting microservice: an 8-stage LangChain analogue-blend pipeline with executor/reviewer factor scoring, a Claude grounding audit that strips hallucinations, and Monte-Carlo-banded uptake curves, producing probabilistic Worst/Base/Best launch forecasts across Vaccines and HIV brands. Per-stage deterministic fallbacks keep output auditable during gateway outages; async jobs stream live progress to a React dashboard.

IMPACT → Brand and launch teams get a defensible demand forecast on demand in minutes instead of a weeks-long manual modelling exercise, directly informing launch and demand-planning decisions.
LangChain GPT + Claude Monte Carlo PostgreSQL
WP-04 · DOCUMENT INTELLIGENCE ~6 HRS/DOC SAVED

Legal Document Intelligence (ICF)

A GenAI tool that verifies site-level Informed Consent Forms against the GSK master template by parsing PDFs with Azure AI Document Intelligence, aligning sections via embeddings + cosine similarity in Azure AI Search, and using GPT-4o to flag missing clauses and deviations from legal procedure. Saves ~6 hours of review per document, compressing a two-week cycle; adopted by legal teams. Built with Celery async processing, a highlighted-deviation PDF viewer, Key Vault, and LangSmith tracing.

IMPACT → Legal reviewers save ~6 hours per document and a two-week cycle collapses to a fast, consistent check, adopted by legal teams as their standard consent-form review.
Azure Doc Intelligence Embeddings + RAG GPT-4o Celery
